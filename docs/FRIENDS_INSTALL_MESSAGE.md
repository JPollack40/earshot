**Your account**

- Password: <a password you set> (you can't change it in the app yet; ask me if you want a new one)

**Install on your phone (about 2 minutes)**
1. On your phone, open https://github.com/JPollack40/earshot/releases/latest
2. Tap the file ending in **.apk** to download it.
3. Open the download. Android will ask to allow installs from your browser (or Files): allow it, go back, tap **Install**.
4. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **More details > Install anyway**. (It's not on the Play Store, so Google doesn't know it.)
5. Open Earshot > **Settings > Sync** > sign in with the details above.
6. Add podcasts with **+**, or import your subscriptions from another app as an OPML file (**+ > Import OPML**).

**In the car (Android Auto)**
Android Auto hides apps that aren't from the Play Store until you allow them. In Earshot: **Settings > Android Auto >
Set up for your car**, and follow the six steps (do it parked; it takes two minutes).

**On a computer**
https://jpollack40.github.io/earshot/ and sign in with the same details.

**Updates**
Earshot checks for updates once a day and shows a banner. Tap **Download** and install it over the old one; your
podcasts stay.
